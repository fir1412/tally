The short version

  • Tally is free. There is no account, subscription or payment, so there is nothing to cancel.
  • It helps you record and understand your own spending. It is not financial, tax or legal advice, and it is not your bank's record.
  • Your data is yours and stays on your device. Keep a backup: if the phone is lost, the app is uninstalled or the browser's data is cleared, Tally cannot recover it.

Check what Tally reads

Receipts and statements are read automatically and can be misread: a total, a date, an item or a category may be wrong. Tally flags what it isn't sure about and checks that items add up, but please check each entry before relying on it, for example for tax relief claims, reimbursements or disputes with a bank. The original receipt or statement is the record.

Business records

Tally is not a bookkeeping system and does not keep the records LHDN requires. If you use a business account, keep your original receipts and records for 7 years.
