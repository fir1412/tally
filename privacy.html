Privacy

English · Bahasa Melayu · 简体中文

Short version: your money data never leaves your phone. The only thing the developer can ever receive is a feedback message you choose to send.

  • No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. Tally is a set of static files; once installed it works offline.
  • Where your data lives: transactions, receipt photos, accounts and settings are stored in your browser's storage on your device (IndexedDB). The developer cannot see it.
  • Receipts are read on your phone by an on-device text reader (PaddleOCR). Photos are never uploaded. Saved photos are re-encoded, which removes location data.
  • Network requests: Tally downloads its own files from GitHub Pages (GitHub may log standard web-server data such as your IP address). If you paste a Google Sheets link, Tally fetches that sheet from Google; nothing else about you is sent.
  • Exchange rates (optional): only when you tap Get today's rate on a foreign-currency account, Tally asks api.frankfurter.dev for that one rate (the European Central Bank's). Nothing about you or your money is sent.
  • Feedback: only if you tap Send feedback, your message, the optional contact you type, and app details (Tally version, phone type, browser, screen width, current screen and language) are sent to the developer through Google Forms. Nothing about your money, receipts or accounts is included. Messages written offline wait on your phone and send when you are back online.
  • Calendar reminders (bills, habits, the daily reminder) are links or files you choose to add to your own calendar; your calendar app keeps them, not Tally.
  • App lock (optional): your PIN is never stored, only a salted hash of it, on your phone. Fingerprint or face is checked by your phone; Tally keeps only an identifier for it and never sees your fingerprint or face. The lock keeps other people out of the app; it does not encrypt your data.
  • Backups and exports are files you create and choose where to keep. Once shared, that service's terms apply.
  • Joint accounts: Share joint accounts makes a file with only the accounts you marked Joint, their entries (and photos if you tick them), joint budgets, the categories they use and the name you chose for joint entries. Nothing from your personal accounts is in it. The file is not encrypted: send it only to your partner, over an app you trust. Tally never sends it by itself.
  • Deleting your data: Settings → Erase everything, or clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app. There is no copy anywhere else, so uninstalling or clearing site data deletes everything: back up first if you want to keep it.

Who makes Tally

Tally is made in Malaysia by one independent developer (fir1412 on GitHub). It is free because there are no servers to pay for: the app is a set of files, your data stays on your phone, so nothing is collected and there is nothing to sell. The source code is public: github.com/tallymy/tallymy.github.io. Questions or problems: Settings → Send feedback.
