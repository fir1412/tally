Privacy

English · Bahasa Melayu · 简体中文 · 繁體中文 · 日本語

Short version: Tally does not send your money data anywhere. It leaves your phone only when you export, back up or share it, or add a bill reminder to Google Calendar. The only thing the developer receives is feedback you choose to send.

  • No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. Tally is a set of static files; once installed it works offline.
  • Where your data lives: transactions, receipt photos, accounts, budgets, bills, categories and settings are stored only on your device, in the storage your browser keeps for the Tally site (IndexedDB). They are not copied to a server or to your other devices. The developer does not receive them.
  • Receipts are read on your phone by an on-device text reader (PaddleOCR). Photos are never uploaded. Saved photos are re-encoded, which removes location data.
  • Network requests: Tally downloads its own files from GitHub Pages (GitHub may log standard web-server data such as your IP address). If you paste a Google Sheets link, Tally fetches that sheet from Google; nothing else about you is sent.
  • Exchange rates (optional): only when you tap Get today's rate on a foreign-currency account, Tally asks api.frankfurter.dev for that one rate (the European Central Bank's). Nothing about you or your money is sent.
  • Feedback: only if you tap Send feedback, your message, the optional contact you type, and app details (Tally version, phone type, browser, screen width, current screen and language) are sent to the developer through Google Forms. Nothing about your money, receipts or accounts is included. Messages written offline wait on your phone and send when you are back online.
  • Calendar reminders (bills, habits, the daily reminder) are a file or a Google Calendar link you choose to add. If you use the Google Calendar link, the reminder's text (for example "Pay Unifi") is sent to Google. Your calendar keeps the reminder, not Tally.
  • App lock (optional): your PIN is never stored, only a salted hash of it, on your phone. Fingerprint or face is checked by your phone; Tally keeps only an identifier for it and never sees your fingerprint or face. The lock keeps other people out of the app. You can also turn on Encrypt data on this phone: your entries and receipt photos are then stored encrypted with your PIN or password (AES-GCM), so they can't be read without it, even from a copy of the phone; your settings (language, text size) stay unencrypted. If you forget the PIN or password, encrypted data can't be recovered.
  • Backups and exports are files you create and choose where to keep. Once shared, that service's terms apply.
  • Joint accounts: Share joint accounts makes a file with only the accounts you marked Joint, their entries (and photos if you tick them), joint budgets, the categories they use and the name you chose for joint entries. Nothing from your personal accounts is in it. The file is not encrypted unless you set a password when you share it: send it only to your partner, over an app you trust. Tally never sends it by itself.
  • Deleting your data: Settings → Erase Tally's data, or clear this site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app. Each deletes only Tally's data on this device (the items listed above); your other apps, photos and files are not touched. There is no copy anywhere else, so back up first if you want to keep it.

Your feedback and your rights

Feedback is used only to fix Tally and to reply to you. Google (Google Forms) stores it for the developer, possibly outside Malaysia, and it is deleted after 12 months. Giving a contact is optional; without it the developer can't reply. Please don't include IC or account numbers. To see, correct or delete feedback you sent, or to withdraw your consent, email fir1412dev@gmail.com, or send a request through Send feedback with a contact; you get an answer within 21 days. If feedback data is ever exposed, the developer will tell the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and the people affected, as the law requires. Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian before adding your contact details.

Who makes Tally

Tally is made in Malaysia by fir1412, one independent developer (fir1412 on GitHub), who is the data user under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 for the feedback you send. It is free because there are no servers to pay for: the app is a set of files, your data stays on your phone, so nothing is collected and there is nothing to sell. The source code is public: github.com/tallymy/tallymy.github.io. Questions, problems or data requests: email fir1412dev@gmail.com, or Settings → Send feedback.
